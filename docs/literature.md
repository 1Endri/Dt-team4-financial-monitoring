### #7 Hazır (2015)
- What: Literature review of analytical models and decision support systems (DSS) for project monitoring and control, focused on Earned Value Analysis.
- Found: EVA covers only time and cost and is mostly used to collect and display data; most project software lacks early-warning functions, and academic models rarely reach practice.
- For FAUnance: Supports our design: an early-warning, user-friendly tool integrated with existing software, with clear monitoring and intervention policies (= our decision-rights table) and variance graphs + tables in the UI.
